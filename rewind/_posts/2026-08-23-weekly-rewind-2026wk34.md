### A Critical Conversation on AI in Librarianship

The delightful & insightful ["Critical Conversation on AI in Librarianship" panel](https://libraryjuiceconnections.org/events/critical-conversation-ai-in-librarianship/), hosted by Sophie from Library Juice Press, deserves its own post. I'll aim to do it justice in the next couple of weeks.

### Magic the Gathering, A Review

If I've ever read a description of certain aspects of experiencing ADHD or generalized anxiety that's as insightful as ["Magic the Gathering, A Review"](https://htmlgiant.com/random/magic-the-gathering-a-review/)'s refrain of "Hon. Stop." and "…sometimes I can feel my hands wishing for cards", I certainly cannot recall it while I type up this note. I haven't played Magic since high school, but this "review" isn't so much about the game as about the author's reasons for finding it compelling.  

### Give the Drummer Some

The URL slug for ["Give the Drummer Some"](https://aeon.co/essays/what-drum-machines-can-teach-us-about-artificial-intelligence) reads "what drum machines can teach us about artificial intelligence", and that's a very apt summary of the piece. If you've ever wanted to read detailed descriptions / close readings of drumming, go ahead and give it a click.
