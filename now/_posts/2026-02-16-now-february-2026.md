# February 2026

Here's a bit of what I've been up to lately (specifically, I'm writing this on 2026-02-19).  

### PhD & Professional Stuff

- participating in a Library Publishing Collective webinar on [Developing AI Policies for Publications](https://youtu.be/6I1Dt1zZnZE?si=yVrd8Af9TKOsfDGV)
- being a guest at the Open Education Network's Tea Time on peer review models
- participating in an Open Education Association webinar on [OER & Title II Accessibility Compliance: Separating Myths from Facts](https://www.opened.org/posts/2026/accessibility-title-ii-and-open-education)
- reading lots from my [[exam-list3-teaching|teaching & instructional design reading list]]
- participating in a Faculty Learning Community on Universal Design for Learning
- collaboratively facilitating a Faculty Learning Community on "Ethical Frameworks for AI"
  - This is part of what's spurred me to add an [`/ai` page](/ai/) to this site.
  - I'm still very keen on avoiding "ethics-washing" regarding these technologies. This means I frequently bring up already-extent consequences of commercial AI, such as what we know from the [NAACP's Stop Dirty Data Centers campaign](https://naacp.org/campaigns/stop-dirty-data-centers) or the [error-prone rollout](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/ai-is-entering-health-care-and-nurses-are-being-asked-to-trust-it/) of AI-generated alerts in health care.
