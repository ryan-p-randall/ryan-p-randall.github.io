    - _Ugly Betty_ has similar overall "wholesome and witty" vibes as both _Jane the Virgin_ and _Ted Lasso_.

It has some surprisingly difficult to watch scenes—to the point where we almost gave up on finishing the season—so I'm very glad it wasn't our introduction to _Star Trek_. Thankfully, so far the second season feels much more like the previous series.
