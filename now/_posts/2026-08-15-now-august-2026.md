## August 2026

It's 2026-08-15, and here's some of what I've been doing.

- progressing more slowly than I'd like through my [exam reading lists]({{ site.url }}/reading/#my-reading-lists), but still making progress!

### Bonus Page Refinement

While writing this post, I noticed & fixed a small accessibility issue with this very page!  

For accessibility and semantic purposes, it's best to have only a [single H1-level heading per page](https://webaim.org/techniques/headings/). However, because this page automatically loads the content of the most recent blog post in my "Now" category, that posts's title automatically produces an H1 level heading on this page as well. So I made a new Jekyll layout that basically duplicates this page's previous layout, with the difference being that this Now page's title is rendered as an H2 (but styled to look like an H1).  

Hurray for continuous improvement!
