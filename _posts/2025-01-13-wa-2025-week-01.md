I use the [ISO weeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_week_date) from Monday to Monday, so ISO Week Week 01 is the week of 2024-12-30/2025-01-05. (Yes, this includes a bit of 2024, as confusing as that is. Time, am I right?)

## Revelation of the Week: Vacation

It had been a very long time since I'd taken a substantial enough vacation to truly disengage from work! I finally dislodged my mind from work over the holidays, though, thankfully.  

Having re-learned the crucial necessity of rest by gaining some distance from work, I'm going to try bringing a better sense of proportion & perspective into the new year.  

I also made some physical progress charts for [my reading lists]({{ site.url }}/reading/#my-reading-lists), hanging them in a place where I pass by them constantly. Seeing this little reminder has already been motivating in a way that feels like self-led accomplishment rather than shame. That's another revelation!

## 100DaysToOffload

Over the break, I also was reminded of the [100 Days to Offload](https://100daystooffload.com/) blogging challenge, via Stefan Bohacek's [Recurring creative challenges site](https://challenges.stefanbohacek.com/#ongoing-events).
