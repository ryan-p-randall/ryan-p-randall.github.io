A second season has been announced, so I'm including those in the number of how many episodes exist.  

In proud Star Trek tradition, this series took a few episodes to really find its footing. By the 5th episode (["Series Acclimation Mil"](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt34127769/?ref_=ttep_ep_5), focused on the holographic character Sam), however, it becomes a delight.  

Even if you're not into Star Trek much at all—or into the more recent series—I'd highly recommend Episode 5 as a potential standalone watch. Its story is largely self-enclosed, with more than sufficient explanation of its connections to the rest of the series to make sense.
